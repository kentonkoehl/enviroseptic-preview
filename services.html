Warning signs of septic failure

From Enviro Septic’s maintenance page. If you’re seeing any of these, call before it turns into a backup in the house.

  • Sluggish drains in the home
  • Plumbing backups
  • Gurgling sounds in the pipes or drains
  • Odors outside
  • Mushy, wet ground around the septic system or waste water lines

How often to pump

Their brochure says a typical system should be inspected and pumped every three to five years. How often yours needs it depends on:

  • How many people live in the house
  • How many bathrooms it has
  • The size of the tank

For a sense of scale, from their site: a 3 bedroom home typically has a 1,250 gallon tank, and a 4 bedroom home a 1,500 gallon tank. Older homes may have much smaller ones.

“Like everything else, septic tanks require maintenance.” Skip it, and sludge overflows into the leach lines and clogs the field, which is the expensive repair.

Dos and don’ts

The short version of the guide Enviro Septic hands out to homeowners.

Do

  • Use water efficiently, and fix leaky faucets and toilets.
  • Keep records of pumpings, repairs, inspections and permits.
  • Learn where your system is, and keep a sketch of it with your records.
  • Plant only grass over and near the system. Roots clog drain fields.

Don’t

  • Flush wipes of any kind, floss, diapers, cat litter, grease or chemicals. “Your system is not a trash can.”
  • Drive or park on any part of the system. It compacts the soil and damages the pipes.
  • Pour caustic drain openers down a clogged drain.
  • Count on additives. They don’t replace pumping.
